\*\*Premium • Playful\*\*

\## Brand Voice



\### Tone

\- Premium but approachable

\- Playful without being childish

\- Warm and inviting

\- Fresh and energetic

\- Confident yet friendly



\### Writing Style

\- Short, conversational copy

\- Light-hearted with subtle wit

\- Focus on experience, comfort, and quality

\- Avoid corporate or overly formal language

\- Celebrate food, community, and everyday moments



\---



\## Visual Direction



\- Rustic textures

\- Warm earthy tones

\- Clean layouts

\- Handcrafted illustrations

\- Soft rounded elements

\- Natural lighting

\- Premium café aesthetic

\- High contrast with generous whitespace
